# New Hire Onboarding

## First Week
New hires collect their laptop from IT at 9:30 AM on the first day and receive a building badge from the front desk. Accounts for email, Slack, and PeopleHub are created before the start date. The manager schedules a welcome lunch during the first week. A buddy from the same team is assigned to every new hire for the first 3 months.

## Probation and Reviews
The probation period lasts 3 months. Managers hold check-ins at 30, 60, and 90 days. Performance reviews take place twice a year, in June and December. Remote work is not available until probation has been completed successfully.

## Mandatory Training
Security awareness training must be completed within the first 14 days. Code of conduct training must be completed within 30 days. Both trainings are repeated every year. Employees who miss a deadline lose access to their accounts until the training is completed.
