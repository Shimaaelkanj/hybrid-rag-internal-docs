# Remote Work Policy

## Eligibility and Schedule
Employees may work remotely up to 3 days per week once they have completed the 3-month probation period. All employees must be online during core hours of 10:00 to 15:00 in their local time zone. Tuesday is the team anchor day, and every team works in the office on Tuesdays. Managers may approve exceptions to the weekly limit for up to 2 weeks at a time.

## Home Office Stipend
Eligible employees receive a one-time home office stipend of $600 for desk, chair, and monitor purchases. In addition, the company pays a monthly internet allowance of $40. Stipend purchases are claimed through ExpensePro, and receipts are required. The claim must be submitted within 60 days of the purchase date.

## Working From Abroad
Employees may work from another country for up to 20 days per calendar year. The request needs approval from both the manager and the Security team, and it must be submitted at least 30 days before departure. Employees must use the company VPN at all times while abroad. Working from countries on the restricted list is not permitted under any circumstances.
