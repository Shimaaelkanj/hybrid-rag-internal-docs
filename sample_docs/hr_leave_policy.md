# Leave Policy

## Annual Leave
Full-time employees receive 24 days of paid annual leave per calendar year. Leave accrues at 2 days per month of service. Up to 5 unused days may be carried over into the next year, and carried-over days expire on March 31. Requests for 5 or more consecutive days must be submitted at least 4 weeks in advance. All leave requests are submitted and approved through the PeopleHub portal.

## Sick Leave
Employees receive 10 paid sick days per calendar year. Sick days do not carry over into the next year. A medical certificate is required when an absence lasts more than 3 consecutive days. Employees must notify their manager before 9:30 AM on the first day of an absence.

## Parental Leave
Primary caregivers receive 16 weeks of parental leave at full salary, and secondary caregivers receive 6 weeks at full salary. Parental leave must be started within 12 months of the birth or adoption. Employees must have completed at least 6 months of service to be eligible. Parental leave is separate from annual leave and sick leave.
