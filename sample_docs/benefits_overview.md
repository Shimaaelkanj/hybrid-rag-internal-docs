# Benefits Overview

## Health Insurance
The company pays 90 percent of the health insurance premium for employees and 70 percent for dependents. Coverage starts on the first day of the month after the employee's start date. Open enrollment takes place every November. Employees may change their plan outside open enrollment only after a qualifying life event.

## Retirement Plan
Northwind offers a 401(k) plan with a 4 percent employer match. New employees are enrolled automatically at a 3 percent contribution rate unless they opt out. Employer contributions vest over 3 years, at one third per year. Employees can change their contribution rate at any time in the benefits portal.

## Wellness and Learning
Every employee receives a wellness stipend of $50 per month for gym or fitness costs. The annual learning budget is $1,200 per employee for courses, books, and conferences. Learning budget purchases need prior manager approval and are claimed through ExpensePro. Unused learning budget does not roll over to the next year.
