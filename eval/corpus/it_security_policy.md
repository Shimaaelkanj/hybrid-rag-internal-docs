# IT Security Policy

## Passwords and MFA
Passwords must be at least 14 characters long and must be changed every 180 days. Multi-factor authentication is mandatory for every employee account and must use an authenticator app rather than SMS. Administrators must use hardware security keys. The company password manager is Vaultline, and storing passwords in browsers is not allowed.

## Device Security
All laptops must have full-disk encryption enabled. Screens must lock automatically after 5 minutes of inactivity. Operating system updates must be installed within 7 days of release. A lost or stolen device must be reported to the Security team within 1 hour of discovery.

## Incident Reporting
Suspected phishing emails should be reported with the Report Phish button in the email client. Security incidents are classified by severity. A SEV1 incident, such as a confirmed data breach, must be acknowledged within 15 minutes. A SEV2 incident must be acknowledged within 1 hour, and a SEV3 incident by the next business day. Urgent incidents are raised in the #sec-incident channel.
