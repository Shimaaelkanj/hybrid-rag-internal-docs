# Expense Policy

## Travel Expenses
Flights must be booked at least 14 days in advance in economy class. The hotel limit is $180 per night, and $250 per night in San Francisco, New York, and London. The daily meal allowance is $60. Personal car mileage is reimbursed at $0.67 per mile.

## Approval Limits
Expenses under $500 need manager approval. Expenses from $500 to $5,000 need director approval. Expenses above $5,000 need approval from a VP and the Finance team. Standard expense claims must be submitted within 30 days of the expense date, and late claims require a CFO exception. The home office stipend is the only exception to the 30-day rule because it has its own 60-day window.

## Client Entertainment
Client meals and entertainment are limited to $100 per person. Every claim must name the client and state the business purpose. Alcohol is reimbursable only when a client is present. All claims are submitted through ExpensePro.
